Mental math, made calm.

nume. is a mental math app for iPhone and iPad. One problem, three choices: answer as many as you can in a minute, take the daily challenge, drill your weak spots, master your times tables, or practise with no clock at all in Zen mode.

No ads, no subscription. Your progress stays on your device.

Support

A question, a bug, an idea? Write to dklsystems@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Restore nume. pro

Open Settings in nume., then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought nume. pro with.

Delete your account

Open Settings in nume., then tap Delete account. Your profile and history are erased from your device straight away.
